27 September 2026 — Version 1.101

Rebuilt a focused Light–Bold family (300, 400, 500, 600, 700) and a matching
300–700 variable font. Retired Thin, ExtraLight, ExtraBold, and Black after
visual review found uneven strokes and disproportionate dots at the extremes.

The new endpoints are derived directly from Regular. Main letter strokes use
consistent offsets; dots follow the stem change in both dimensions while
retaining their optical centers. Paired dots spread symmetrically for reading
size clarity. Diagonal and curved accents use a separate, consistent profile.
Added binary measurements of stem consistency, dot/stem ratios, dot centers,
dieresis gaps, and accent proportions, alongside 16/24/48 px visual proofs.

27 September 2026 — Version 1.100 (superseded)

Added an algorithmically derived upright weight family from Thin (100) to
Black (900), with nine static TTFs, a variable wght TTF, and matching WOFF2s.
The original Regular design is the reference; no additional designer-authored
masters were present in the repository. Generation preserves compatible cubic
point topology and applies local limits to protect counters and joins.

Corrected uppercase Ukrainian Yi (U+0407): its source component referenced I
without a dieresis; it now references Idieresis. Advance widths are unchanged.
Corrected Lcaron (U+013D) and tcaron (U+0165), whose comma-shaped carons were
incorrectly placed below the baseline. They now sit in the upper-right position.

Added reproducible source generation, locked build dependencies, a FontBakery
Universal check, OpenType Sanitizer validation, static/variable equivalence
checks, Latin/Cyrillic shaping checks, and complete glyph proofs.
